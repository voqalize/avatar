Blind lipsync evaluation

Watch the mouth, guess the words, then reveal. Knowing the text first makes almost any lipsync look convincing — that bias is what this page exists to avoid.

What to look for

  1. Do closed-mouth moments land on p / b / m?
  2. Does the mouth move at all during vowels, or flap uniformly?
  3. Does it start and stop with the voice, or lag?
  4. On the interjections — is "Okay" distinguishable from "Sure"?
