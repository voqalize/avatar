What to look for

  1. Do the closed mouths (A) land on p / b / m?
  2. Does the mouth move through vowels, or flap uniformly?
  3. Does it start and stop with the voice, or hang open at the end?
  4. In the commas: does the mouth come to rest, or stay parked on a vowel? Step Rest with r at 0.25x on ev_22 — 0 is Rhubarb's own rule and holds an open shape through three of that clip's four pauses, then opens once more on a lip smack after the last word. Too low a threshold is a real failure too: watch for it snapping shut inside a long p or t closure and reading as a stutter.
  5. On text: does it finish early? That is the duration estimate, not the shapes — check fit to confirm.
  6. If everything is uniformly early or late, that is not the shapes either — null it with Offset before judging anything else. On audio, mouth-open onset is measured to land within one 10 ms frame of speech onset on all ten clips, so a whole-track shift you can see is coming from playback, not from the cues. The offset that nulls it should be close to the reported device latency; headphones over Bluetooth need 150–300 ms and speakers ~30 ms.
